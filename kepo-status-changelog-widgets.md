# Keep SaaS status pages and changelogs on your Mac desktop

Product and engineering folks often bounce between status pages, roadmap blogs and GitHub release feeds. Each hop is another tab and another context switch.

With [Kepo](https://kepo.ai) you can turn those same URLs and RSS feeds into live Mac desktop widgets. The panel stays out of the way until you summon it, so incident noise and release notes stay visible without living in the browser.

## Suggested layout
- Status / incident feeds for the three services you depend on most.
- One changelog widget for your own product or a key vendor.
- One optional HN / indie-hackers digest if you still want industry noise — kept separate from work widgets.

This remake note is intentionally short: pick three widgets, leave the rest of the screen empty, and revisit in a week.
