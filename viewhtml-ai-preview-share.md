# Preview AI-generated HTML before you share it

ChatGPT, Claude and coding agents happily emit HTML snippets. Pasting them straight into Slack or email without a visual check is how layout bugs escape.

[ViewHTML](https://viewhtml.app) is a free online HTML preview: paste markup, see a live render, then share a link, QR, PNG or PDF. No signup.

## A simple AI → review loop
1. Generate the HTML in your agent or chat.
2. Paste into [viewhtml.app](https://viewhtml.app) and fix obvious CSS/structure issues.
3. Share the preview link with a teammate or client instead of a raw code dump.

Works well for landing sections, email-ish markup experiments and single-file prototypes that do not deserve a full deploy yet.
