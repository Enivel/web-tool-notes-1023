# RSS and market feeds as calm Mac desktop widgets

Browser tabs are a noisy way to watch the same five feeds all day. A quieter pattern is to pin RSS, social and market sources as desktop widgets and glance at them only when you need a status check.

[Kepo](https://kepo.ai) is a Mac app built for that: turn feeds and small scripts into widgets in one floating panel, summoned with a global shortcut. You can also describe a new widget in plain language and let AI scaffold it.

## A practical starter pack
1. One RSS widget for two or three trusted sources (not twenty).
2. One markets widget with the tickers you actually care about.
3. One "work" widget — GitHub releases, a status page, or a changelog.

Review the pack weekly and delete anything you have not looked at. The goal is fewer distractions, not a second dashboard.
